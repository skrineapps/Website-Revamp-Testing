Social Responsibility

Skrine for the Greater Good: giving back to the communities where we live and work.

Giving

Skrine for the Greater Good

A responsibility we take seriously

As one of the oldest law firms in the country, Skrine believes it has a responsibility to contribute to civil society and nation-building.

Through our social responsibility initiatives, our people volunteer their time and skills to improve the lives of the communities in which we live and operate.

Our initiatives

Where we give our time

  1. Skrine x Project ID

    Since 2021, workshops for young people on public speaking, presentation and negotiation skills, CV writing and interviews, mediation, knowing your rights, and career and role-model sharing.

  2. Pertiwi Soup Kitchen

    Three volunteer drives in 2022, packing and serving meals with the Pertiwi Soup Kitchen.

  3. Skrine x TFM

    Running together at the Kuala Lumpur Standard Chartered Marathon 2022, and sharing our knowledge at TFM's We Think Digital.

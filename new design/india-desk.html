Arbitration in Malaysia

Why resolve disputes here

As commercial activity between Malaysia and India expands, parties increasingly seek efficient and neutral forums for resolving cross-border disputes. The India Desk advises on dispute resolution strategies that make effective use of:

  1. 01

    Modern arbitration framework

  2. 02

    Specialist judiciary

  3. 03

    Established institutional arbitration infrastructure

  4. 04

    Strong record in enforcing arbitral awards

  5. 05

    Procedural flexibility

  6. 06

    Legal certainty

  7. 07

    Competitive cost structures

Together, these position Malaysia as a compelling forum for international arbitration in the region.

The Desk also keeps clients informed of relevant legal developments, policy shifts and enforcement trends, supporting sound transaction structuring, risk management and dispute preparedness.

Skrine Dragons

Partners, associates, staff and pupils in one boat, paddling together since 2005.

Crew

Our crew

One boat. Every level of the firm.

“The only dragon boat team backed by a legal firm in Malaysia.”

The Skrine Dragons bring the whole firm together: partners, associates, staff and pupils train, travel and race side by side. Rank stays on the shore. In the boat, everyone pulls together.

Since forming in 2005 the crew has raced across Malaysia and the region, and was the only Malaysian team at the IDBF 8th Club Crew World Championships.

Skrine Regatta: Paddling for Charity

Sports & Social

Good teams are built outside the office too. Fun is how we build our team spirit.

Fun

Work hard, play together

Fun is part of how we work

We believe a team that enjoys time together works better together. That is why our calendar is full of trips, socials, sport and celebrations, open to everyone in the firm, and often to their families too.

Above all, the Annual Dinner is a firm tradition: the night we celebrate our culture and our heritage together, alongside Founders' Day and our firm anniversaries.

On the calendar

Something for everyone

  1. Company trips

    Every two years, the whole firm travels together.

  2. Family Day

    An annual day out with the families behind our people.

  3. Lawyers' socials

    Quarterly get-togethers across practice groups.

  4. Team-building

    Activities that turn colleagues into teammates.

  5. Mini dragon boat races

    A taste of the Skrine Dragons for everyone.

  6. Sports club

    Volleyball, netball, bowling and more.

  7. Inter-floor competitions

    Friendly rivalry between floors, all in good fun.

  8. Annual Dinner

    Our tradition celebrating the firm's culture and heritage.

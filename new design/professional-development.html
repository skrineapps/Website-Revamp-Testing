Professional Development

Learning never stops at Skrine, from your first day as a pupil to your years as a partner.

Learn

Our programme

Built by partners, for every lawyer and pupil

Our Professional Development programme is overseen by a committee of partners and supported by a legally qualified Knowledge Management Officer. It is open to all our lawyers and pupils.

Talks cover the full range of our practice, from new legislation and recent cases to the practical skills new lawyers need to hit the ground running.

Three pillars

How we keep growing

  1. In-house legal talks

    At least two talks every month, led by our own partners and lawyers.

  2. Bar Council accreditation

    The programme is accredited by the Bar Council Malaysia for CPD points.

  3. International conferences

    Partners and senior lawyers attend conferences worldwide each year and bring back what they learn to share with the firm.

Overview

About the China Desk

Skrine has been providing quality legal services to Chinese clients for a long time, and established the China Desk early to connect with and serve important Chinese clients and corporations.

Who we are

Skrine was established on 1 May 1963 and is now one of the largest integrated law firms in Malaysia. The firm is committed to actively providing customers with practical, efficient and comprehensive legal services and solutions. Skrine is Lex Mundi's only member in Malaysia. Lex Mundi is a network of independent law firms with members in 160 regions around the world.

Our clients

Skrine's clients include Malaysian and foreign, international and multinational corporations, from sectors including trade, finance, commercial banking, investment banking, telecommunications, insurance, multimedia, construction, electronics, mining, oil and gas, aerospace, shipping, pharmaceuticals and many more. Skrine is also a pioneer law firm in Malaysia's Financial and Islamic Finance Law, and one of three law firms recognised by the National Bank of Malaysia and the International Islamic Financial Center Malaysia Network.

Along the Belt and Road

We deeply understand the significance of Malaysia's strategic geographical location and have long recruited multilingual professionals. With the implementation of China's Belt and Road Initiative, Malaysia, with its strategic location along the Maritime Road, rich culture and large Chinese community, offers excellent opportunities for Chinese corporations to invest in Malaysia.

A bilingual team

With the strategic vision of our leading partners, the support of our Chinese clients, and our persons in charge's deep understanding of Chinese corporate culture and ways of working, the China Desk has grown into a strong and efficient team. Members are fluent in both Mandarin and English, and are drawn from different practice groups to communicate effectively and serve Chinese clients.

Seminars in Mandarin

Over the years, the China Desk has organised and co-hosted legal and investment seminars with Malaysian, foreign and international networks, chambers of commerce, NGOs and associations. Conducted in Mandarin, they have been very well received by Chinese corporations, making the Desk one of the leading China Desk practices among Malaysian law firms and a supporter of China's Belt and Road Initiative.

关于思纪龄

思纪龄律师事务所成立于1963年5月1日，现为马来西亚规模最大的综合性律师事务所之一。本所致力于为客户提供实际、高效率及全方位的法律服务和解决方案。思纪龄是Lex Mundi在马来西亚的唯一成员。Lex Mundi是一个国际性的独立律师事务所网络，其成员遍布全球160个区域。

我们的客户

思纪龄的客户包括了马来西亚国内及国外、国际及跨国企业。客户来自各个领域和行业，其中包括贸易、金融、商业银行、投资银行、电讯、保险、多媒体、建筑、电子、矿业、石油与天然气、航空、船务、药剂业和其他各式各样行业。思纪龄也是马来西亚金融法和回教金融法领域的先驱律师事务所，更是马来西亚国家银行和马来西亚国际回教金融中心网络所认可的三所律师事务所之一。

"一带一路"上的马来西亚

01 Life at Skrine

Skrine Dragons

“The only dragon boat team backed by a legal firm in Malaysia.”

Since 2005, partners, associates, staff and pupils have trained, travelled and raced side by side. The crew has paddled in Penang, Sabah, Malacca, Hong Kong and Boracay, and was the only Malaysian team at the IDBF 8th Club Crew World Championships.

Every two years we host the Skrine Regatta: Paddling for Charity, where our clients join the race to raise funds for good causes.
